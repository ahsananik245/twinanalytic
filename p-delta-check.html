Compliance Check · BNBC 2020 Sec 2.5.7.9

P-Delta Stability Coefficient Check

Evaluates the stability coefficient θ at every storey from the gravity load, storey shear, storey height and the amplified drift. The limiting θ is the code minimum of 0.5/(βCd) and 0.25.

Design Parameters

System Factors
θmax = 0.5 / (β · Cd), capped at 0.25. Take β = 1.0 when the storey shear capacity is not explicitly computed — that is the conservative default.
