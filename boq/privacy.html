QUANTIX Privacy Policy

QUANTIX by TwinAnalytic · Effective 3 October 2026

QUANTIX is a building quantity and cost estimating app published by TwinAnalytic. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the QUANTIX Android app or the web app at twinanalytic.com/boq.

Summary

QUANTIX is free. Your projects, ledgers and settings stay on your device, and there are no analytics. If you choose to sign in with Google, they are also kept in a private folder in your own Google Drive so you can use them on other devices (see "Google sign-in and Drive sync"); TwinAnalytic never receives them. The Android app shows a short ad only when you download a PDF or Excel file (see "Advertising" below). The web version shows no ads.

Advertising

In the Android app, ads are provided by Google AdMob and appear only on the download screen: a short ad before a PDF or Excel download, or an optional video ad if you choose a copy without the TwinAnalytic watermark. To show and measure ads, Google may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address and information about how you interact with the ad. TwinAnalytic does not receive this data. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android Settings → Google → Ads. Where the law requires it (for example in the EEA and UK), the app asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown.

Data stored on your device

Your projects, calculator inputs, rates and settings are saved on your device (in the app's local storage). They leave your device only if you export or share them, or turn on Google Drive sync. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its data, deletes them. On Android, the operating system's own backup feature may include this data in your personal Google backup if you have enabled it; that backup is controlled by your Google account settings, not by TwinAnalytic.

Google sign-in and Drive sync (optional)

In Settings you can sign in with your Google account to keep your projects, construction ledgers and tenders on all your devices. QUANTIX then asks Google for your name and email address (to show which account is signed in) and for access to its own hidden app folder in your Google Drive (the drive.appdata permission). The app can read and write only that folder, never your other Drive files. Your data goes directly between your device and Google's servers over an encrypted connection; TwinAnalytic has no server for it and cannot see it. Google's privacy policy applies to data stored in your Drive.

You can sign out at any time in Settings; syncing stops and nothing is deleted. To remove the data from Drive, open drive.google.com → Settings → Manage apps → QUANTIX → Delete hidden app data. You can also remove QUANTIX's access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Subscriptions (QUANTIX Pro)

QUANTIX Pro is bought in the Android app through Google Play. Google handles the payment; TwinAnalytic never receives your card or bank details. The app asks Google Play which subscriptions your account has, to unlock Pro features. If you use Google Drive sync, the app also saves which plan is active in its private Drive folder so the web version can unlock Pro for the same account. Manage or cancel the subscription in Google Play.

Construction ledgers

Ledger entries (purchases, payments, suppliers and their phone numbers) are stored on your device, and in your own Google Drive only if you turn on sync. When you share a ledger, the file goes only to the app or person you choose in the share sheet.

Notifications

Bill reminders are scheduled on your phone by the app itself. Nothing is sent to a server, and you can turn them off in Settings.

Files you export

When you export a BOQ or calculation as Excel, PDF or a backup file, the file is created on your device and handed to the destination you pick in the Android share sheet or your browser (for example email, WhatsApp or Google Drive). That destination's own privacy policy then applies.

Rate updates

About once a week, and when you tap "Check for new PWD rates", the app downloads small files from twinanalytic.com to see whether a new PWD Schedule of Rates or new material market prices have been published. The request contains no personal data and nothing about your projects; like any web request, it reveals your IP address to the website's host for delivery.

Permissions

The Android app requests the standard Internet permission (the app's web view and optional rate updates), permission to show notifications (bill reminders, only if you allow it), to run at start-up so scheduled reminders survive a phone restart, and the advertising ID used by Google AdMob for the download-screen ads. QUANTIX works fully offline (rate updates are optional) and does not access your location, contacts, camera, microphone or photos.

Children

QUANTIX is a professional tool for engineers and builders and is not directed at children.
