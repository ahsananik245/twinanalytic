QUANTIX Privacy Policy

QUANTIX by TwinAnalytic · Effective 2 October 2026

QUANTIX is a building quantity and cost estimating app published by TwinAnalytic. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the QUANTIX Android app or the web app at twinanalytic.com/boq.

Summary

QUANTIX does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal data. There is no account, no sign-in, no advertising and no analytics.

Data stored on your device

Your projects, calculator inputs, rates and settings are saved only on your device (in the app's local storage). They never leave your device unless you choose to export or share them. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its data, deletes them. On Android, the operating system's own backup feature may include this data in your personal Google backup if you have enabled it; that backup is controlled by your Google account settings, not by TwinAnalytic.

Construction ledgers

Ledger entries (purchases, payments, suppliers and their phone numbers) are stored only on your device. When you share a ledger, the file goes only to the app or person you choose in the share sheet.

Files you export

When you export a BOQ or calculation as Excel, PDF or a backup file, the file is created on your device and handed to the destination you pick in the Android share sheet or your browser (for example email, WhatsApp or Google Drive). That destination's own privacy policy then applies.

Rate updates

About once a week, and when you tap "Check for new PWD rates", the app downloads small files from twinanalytic.com to see whether a new PWD Schedule of Rates or new material market prices have been published. The request contains no personal data and nothing about your projects; like any web request, it reveals your IP address to the website's host for delivery.

Permissions

The Android app requests only the standard Internet permission, which the app's web view requires. QUANTIX works fully offline (rate updates are optional) and does not access your location, contacts, camera, microphone or photos.

Children

QUANTIX is a professional tool for engineers and builders and is not directed at children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions about this policy: please get in touch via twinanalytic.com/contact.
