Compliance Check · BNBC 2020 Table 6.2.21

Storey Drift, Drift Ratio & Sway Limitation

Three checks in one pass: the design storey drift against the occupancy allowance, the dimensionless drift ratio, and total sway against H/500. The allowance is a fraction of the storey height, not the total building height.

System Factors
Storey heights are entered in metres and displacements in millimetres. The allowable storey drift is the ratio times the storey height, per BNBC Table 6.2.21.
