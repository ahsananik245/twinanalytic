Load Analysis · BNBC 2020 Sec 2.7

BNBC 2020 Load Combination Generator

Evaluates the full set of strength design combinations for one load effect — an axial force, a moment or a shear. The vertical earthquake effect shifts the dead load factor in the seismic combinations, and the uplift case is reported alongside the maximum.
