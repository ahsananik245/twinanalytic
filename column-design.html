Page 1: Scope & General Requirements (10.1 – 10.2)

Applicability

  • Chapter 10 applies to reinforced concrete columns that are part of a structural system.
  • Cast-in-place, precast, and composite columns.
  • Subject to axial load, moment, shear, and torsion.
  • Must satisfy strength, serviceability, durability, and integrity (Chapter 4).

Column Types

  • Tied Columns: Longitudinal bars enclosed by separate ties. Most common for building columns.
  • Spiral Columns: Longitudinal bars enclosed by a continuous spiral. Better ductility and confinement.
  • Composite Columns: Structural steel shapes or pipes embedded in concrete.

Slenderness & Stability

All columns must be checked for slenderness effects per Chapter 6:

  • kℓu / r ≤ 22 → Nonsway, slenderness may be neglected
  • kℓu / r ≤ 34 + 12(M₁/M₂) → Braced columns
  • If exceeded: use Moment Magnification (6.6.4) or Second-Order Analysis (6.7)

Design Limits (10.3)

  • Minimum dimension: 8 in. or as required for cover
  • Minimum eccentricity: M₂,min = Pᵤ(0.6 + 0.03h)
  • Must accommodate concrete placement
  • Seismic: Chapter 18 additional requirements

Page 2: Required Strength & Load Combinations (10.4 / Chapter 5)

Load Combinations

Equation Load Combination Primary Load
(5.3.1a) U = 1.4 D Dead
(5.3.1b) U = 1.2 D + 1.6 L + 0.5(Lᵣ or S or R) Live
(5.3.1c) U = 1.2 D + 1.6(Lᵣ or S or R) + (1.0L or 0.5W) Roof/Live/Snow
(5.3.1d) U = 1.2 D + 1.0 W + 1.0 L + 0.5(Lᵣ or S or R) Wind
(5.3.1e) U = 1.2 D + 1.0 E + 1.0 L + 0.2 S Seismic
(5.3.1f) U = 0.9 D + 1.0 W Wind (overturning)
(5.3.1g) U = 0.9 D + 1.0 E Seismic (overturning)

Column Force Components

  • Pᵤ = Factored axial force (positive for compression, negative for tension)
  • Mᵤ = Factored moment (include minimum eccentricity and second-order effects)
  • Vᵤ = Factored shear force (critical for moment frame columns)

Minimum Eccentricity (6.6.4.5.4)

M₂,min = Pᵤ (0.6 + 0.03 h)
h = column dimension in direction of bending (in.)

Second-Order Effects

If kℓᵤ/r exceeds limits:

  • Moment Magnification (6.6.4)
  • Second-Order Elastic Analysis (6.7)

Page 3: Design Strength & φ-Factors (10.5 / Chapter 21)

Basic Strength Requirement

φ Sₙ ≥ U
Design Strength ≥ Required Strength

Strength-Reduction Factors for Columns (21.2)

Condition Tied Column φ Spiral Column φ
Compression-controlled (εₜ ≤ εₜᵧ) 0.65 0.75
Transition zone 0.65 → 0.90 0.75 → 0.90
Tension-controlled (εₜ ≥ 0.005) 0.90 0.90

Nominal Strength Calculation — Interaction Diagram Method (Strain Compatibility)

  1. Assume neutral axis depth c
  2. Calculate concrete stress block depth: a = β₁ c
  3. Determine steel strains: εₛ = 0.003 × (d - c) / c
  4. Compute steel stresses: fₛ = Eₛ εₛ (≤ fᵧ)
  5. Sum forces: Pₙ = C꜀ + ΣCₛ - ΣTₛ
  6. Sum moments about centroid: Mₙ = Pₙ × e

β₁ Factor

β₁ = 0.85 for f'c ≤ 4,000 psi
β₁ = 0.85 - 0.05(f'c - 4000)/1000  (minimum 0.65)

Pure Axial Capacity

P₀ = 0.85 f'c (Aɢ - Aₛₜ) + fᵧ Aₛₜ
Pₙ,max = 0.80 P₀  (tied)
Pₙ,max = 0.85 P₀  (spiral)

Interaction Check

For every load combination (Pᵤ, Mᵤ), the design point must lie inside the φPₙ–φMₙ curve.

Page 4: Reinforcement Limits (10.6 / Chapter 25)

Longitudinal Reinforcement

  • Minimum: Aₛ,min = 0.01 Aɢ (1% of gross area)
  • Maximum: Aₛ,max = 0.08 Aɢ (8% of gross area)

Practical Limits

  • ≤ 4% when bars are not spliced
  • 6%–8% only with splices and proper concrete placement
  • Minimum 4 longitudinal bars for rectangular tied columns
  • Minimum 6 longitudinal bars for circular spiral columns

Bar Spacing & Cover (25.2, 20.5)

sₘᵢₙ = max( 1.0 in., dᵦ, 4/3 dₐɢɢ )
  • Interior, not exposed: 1.5 in. cover
  • Exposed to weather or in contact with ground: 2.0 in.

Bundled Bars (25.6)

  • Permitted if bars are tied, wired, or secured in contact
  • Bundles of 3 or 4 bars allowed with adequate confinement
  • Development length increased by 20% for 3-bar, 33% for 4-bar

Development & Splices

  • Development length ℓd per 25.4
  • Lap splices: Class A (1.0ℓd) or Class B (1.3ℓd) per 25.5.2
  • Mechanical splices per 25.5.3 (Type 1 or 2)
  • Welded splices per 25.5.4

Page 5: Tied Column Detailing (10.7 / 25.7.2)

Tie Size (25.7.2.2)

  • No. 3 ties for longitudinal bars ≤ No. 10
  • No. 4 ties for longitudinal bars > No. 10 or bundled bars

Tie Spacing (25.7.2.1)

s ≤ min( 16 dᵦ,  48 dᵦ,ₜᵢₑ,  least column dimension )

Tie Arrangement Rules (25.7.2.3)

  • Every corner bar and alternate bar shall have lateral support by a tie corner or seismic hook.
  • No unsupported bar shall be farther than 6 in. clear from a supported bar.
  • Ties shall be arranged so that no bar is more than s/2 from a laterally supported bar.
  • For seismic-force-resisting systems (SDC C–F), use seismic hooks and crossties per Chapter 18.

Vertical Spacing at Floor Levels

  • First tie not more than s/2 above top of footing or slab
  • Last tie not more than s/2 below lowest reinforcement in supported member
  • Ties placed vertically as close as practicable to slab soffits
  • Uniform spacing unless required closer in splice zones

Page 6: Spiral Column Detailing (10.7 / 25.7.3)

Spiral Bar Requirements

  • Minimum bar size: No. 3 (3/8 in.) diameter
  • Spirals shall be continuous, of regular pitch, and properly anchored

Spiral Pitch (Clear Spacing)

1.0 in. ≤ s ≤ 3.0 in.

Minimum Volumetric Ratio (25.7.3.3)

ρₛ ≥ 0.45 (Aɢ / A꜀ₕ - 1) (f'c / fᵧₜ)

Aɢ = gross area, A꜀ₕ = core area, fᵧₜ = spiral yield

Spiral Advantages

  • Higher φ factor: 0.75 vs 0.65 for tied columns
  • Better confinement increases concrete strength and ductility
  • Preferred for high seismic zones (SDC D, E, F)
  • Core remains intact after cover spalls under extreme load

Anchorage & Splices

  • Spirals may be spliced by lapping, welding, or mechanical anchorage
  • Lap splices: 1.5 turn minimum overlap for deformed wire/bar
  • Anchorage at ends: 1.5 extra turns at top and bottom

Page 7: Step-by-Step Design Procedure

Step Title Key Actions
1 Design Data Establish f'c, fᵧ, column shape, story height, boundary conditions. Determine Pᵤ, Mᵤ, Vᵤ.
2 Slenderness Check Compute kℓᵤ/r. If ≤ 22 (nonsway) or ≤ 34+12(M₁/M₂) (braced), neglect. Otherwise, moment magnification or second-order analysis.
3 Minimum Eccentricity Calculate M₂,min = Pᵤ(0.6 + 0.03h). Use larger of analysis moment or M₂,min.
4 Trial Section & Reinforcement Choose dimensions (b, h) and longitudinal bars. Check 0.01 ≤ ρ ≤ 0.08.
5 Determine φ Factor Assume compression-controlled (φ = 0.65 tied, 0.75 spiral). Verify εₜ after calculating nominal strength.
6 Interaction Diagram Use strain compatibility: vary c, compute strains, stresses, Pₙ, Mₙ. Plot φPₙ vs φMₙ and verify design point lies inside curve.
7 Pure Axial Check Pₙ,max = 0.80P₀ (tied) or 0.85P₀ (spiral). Ensure φPₙ,max ≥ Pᵤ.
8 Transverse Reinforcement Ties: size, spacing, arrangement per 25.7.2. Spirals: pitch, ratio ρₛ, anchorage per 25.7.3.
9 Detailing Checks Cover, bar spacing, development length, splices (Chapter 25). Seismic detailing per Chapter 18 if applicable.
10 Construction Documents Show column size, bar marks, tie/spiral size & spacing, splice locations, and cover.

Page 8: Worked Example — 18" × 18" Tied Column

Given Data
- Column: 18" × 18" (Aɢ = 324 in²)
- f'c = 4,000 psi | fᵧ = 60,000 psi
- Pᵤ = 500 kips | Mᵤ = 250 k-ft
- ℓᵤ = 12 ft | Braced (nonsway), k = 1.0

Step 1: Slenderness Check

r = 0.30 × 18 = 5.4 in.
kℓᵤ / r = 1.0 × 144 / 5.4 = 26.7
Limit = 34 + 12(0) = 34
26.7 < 34  →  OK (slenderness neglected)

Step 2: Minimum Eccentricity

M₂,min = Pᵤ(0.6 + 0.03h) = 500 × (0.6 + 0.54) = 570 k-in = 47.5 k-ft
Applied Mᵤ = 250 k-ft > 47.5 k-ft  →  Use Mᵤ = 250 k-ft

Step 3: Select Reinforcement

Try 8 No. 8 bars: Aₛₜ = 8 × 0.79 = 6.32 in²
ρ = 6.32 / 324 = 1.95%  →  1% < 1.95% < 8%  OK

Step 4: Pure Axial Capacity (φ = 0.65 assumed)

P₀ = 0.85 × 4 × (324 - 6.32) + 60 × 6.32 = 1,080 + 379 = 1,459 kips
Pₙ,max = 0.80 × 1,459 = 1,167 kips
φPₙ,max = 0.65 × 1,167 = 758 kips > Pᵤ = 500 kips  OK

Step 5: Interaction Check (Strain Compatibility)

Required Pₙ = Pᵤ / φ = 500 / 0.65 = 769 kips
Required Mₙ = Mᵤ / φ = 250 / 0.65 = 385 k-ft = 4,620 k-in
Using c = 10.5 in. (compression-controlled, εₜ < εₜᵧ):
Pₙ = 1,050 kips,  Mₙ = 520 k-ft
φPₙ = 683 kips > 500 kips  OK
φMₙ = 338 k-ft > 250 k-ft  OK

Step 6: Tie Design

Tie size: No. 3 (longitudinal bars are No. 8 ≤ No. 10)
Spacing: min(16×1.0=16", 48×0.375=18", 18") = 16"
Use No. 3 ties @ 12" o.c. (practical, within limit)
Arrangement: Square tie supporting all 8 bars

Step 7: Detailing Checks

Cover: 1.5" to ties (interior, not exposed)  OK
Clear spacing: (18 - 2×1.5 - 2×0.375 - 1.0)/2 = 6.6" > 1.0"  OK

Page 9: Interaction Diagram & Design Verification

Column: 18" × 18" | f'c = 4 ksi | fᵧ = 60 ksi | 8 No. 8 bars

The interaction diagram plots φPₙ vs φMₙ. The design point (250 k-ft, 500 k) lies inside the curve.

Verification Summary

  • φPₙ = 683 kips > Pᵤ = 500 kips OK
  • φMₙ = 338 k-ft > Mᵤ = 250 k-ft OK
  • φPₙ,max = 758 kips > Pᵤ = 500 kips OK
