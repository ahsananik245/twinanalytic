Member Design · ACI Method 2 / BNBC 2020

Two-Way Slab Design — Moment Coefficient Method

Designs a two-way slab supported on stiff beams along all four edges. Reads the ACI Method 2 coefficients for any of the nine panel cases, sets the minimum thickness from the beam-to-slab stiffness ratio, and schedules top and bottom steel in both directions.

Design Parameters

Design Method & Panel
Under USD both the negative and the positive moments carry their load factors. Splitting the positive moment into dead and live parts does not remove the factors.
Materials
Panel Geometry
A and B are clear spans face to face of the supporting beams, which is what the ACI coefficient method is calibrated on.
Loading
Wall Load on the Panel
