## Video direction

Charcoal throughout — the site's own `#131313`, never a lighter deck background. The
only structural device is a 1px hairline in dim warm gold; no boxes, no shadows, no
cards with borders. Playfair Display for the spoken lines, Sora for supporting text,
JetBrains Mono for every number and every code reference, matching twinanalytic.com so
the video and the site read as one thing.

Motion is slow and mechanical rather than bouncy. Nothing overshoots, nothing springs.
Reveals settle on long-tail curves. The subject is precision, and motion that shows off
would argue against the message.

Rhythm across the six frames: **move, build, move, hold, move, rest.** Frame 4 is the
deliberate held beat — the real calculation output stays still and is allowed to be
read, which is the whole point of it being there. Frame 6 rests on the address.

Screenshots are the site as it actually is. They are dimmed and pushed, never
recomposed, and no interface element is faked or rebuilt.
