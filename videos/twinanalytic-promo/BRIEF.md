## Intent

A promotional video for twinanalytic.com, a new structural engineering and BIM
consultancy working to BNBC 2020 and ACI 318. It goes on the website and travels
with outreach — sent directly to architects, contractors and developers who may
not have heard of the firm yet.

The firm is new and has no completed case studies it can publish, so the video
must not imply a track record it does not have. What it does have is unusual and
provable: twenty-eight structural calculators, free and open with no sign-up, and
two published worked examples where every number can be reproduced by the reader.
That is the story — competence demonstrated rather than asserted.

Tone: precise, quiet, technical. Engineers are the audience and overclaiming
reads as a warning sign to them. Closer to a well-set calculation sheet than a
startup promo. No stock imagery of hard hats or handshakes.

## Assets

The captured site provides the visual material. The strongest real screens, in
order of usefulness:

- The home hero — an animated wireframe structural frame that assembles itself
- The calculator suite index — visible proof of the twenty-eight tools
- A calculator mid-result — real numbers on screen, not a mockup
- The worked example pages — tables of real derived values

## Customizations

- Silent-capable: on-screen text must carry the whole message on its own, since
  much of the audience will watch muted in a feed. Narration supports it rather
  than replacing it.
- No invented clients, testimonials, project photos, logos or statistics. Nothing
  on screen that is not true today.
- Real screenshots of the live site wherever a product shot is needed. Do not
  rebuild the pages as stylised HTML.

## Notes

- Brand: warm charcoal (#131313) with a silver-to-gold metallic accent (#C9A84C).
  Serif display face (Playfair Display), sans body (Sora), mono for numbers
  (JetBrains Mono). The site's own palette — the video should look like the site.
- Codes to name accurately: BNBC 2020 for loading and seismic, ACI 318 for member
  design. Getting a code reference wrong would undo the credibility the video is
  built on.
- The calculators are genuinely free and need no sign-up to run. Details are asked
  for only when downloading the PDF report. Do not say "free trial" or "sign up".
- Closing action: visit twinanalytic.com. Not "book a demo" — there is no demo.
