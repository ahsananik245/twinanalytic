# SCRIPT — twinanalytic-promo

**Voice:** am_michael (Kokoro, local)
**Voice direction:** Measured and unhurried. The audience is engineers, so certainty
should come from the facts rather than from emphasis. Read it the way you would read a
calculation out to a colleague — level, slightly dry, no sell.

---

## Line 1 — The premise (Frame 1)

**Time:** 0.0 – 4.5s
**Delivery:** Plain and flat. A statement of fact, not a hook to be sold.

    Every building starts as a set of numbers that have to be right.

## Line 2 — Who this is (Frame 2)

**Time:** 4.5 – 9.5s
**Delivery:** Even pace. Let the two code names land as separate facts.

    TwinAnalytic is a structural engineering and BIM consultancy, working to BNBC 2020 and ACI 318.

## Line 3 — The calculators (Frame 3)

**Time:** 9.5 – 15.5s
**Delivery:** Slight lift on the number, then straight. "Free" needs no selling tone.

    Twenty-eight structural calculators. Free, and open without signing up.

## Line 4 — The working is visible (Frame 4)

**Time:** 15.5 – 22.0s
**Delivery:** The longest line — keep it moving, but do not rush the last clause.

    Seismic, wind, member design and detailing, each one showing every step, against the clause it comes from.

## Line 5 — Worked examples (Frame 5)

**Time:** 22.0 – 27.0s
**Delivery:** This is the line that replaces a case study. Say it like an invitation to check.

    And two complete worked examples, where every number can be reproduced.

## Line 6 — The address (Frame 6)

**Time:** 27.0 – 30.0s
**Delivery:** Quiet. One instruction, no upsell.

    TwinAnalytic dot com.
