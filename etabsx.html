EtabsX handles the repetitive, error-prone parts of building an ETABS model to the Bangladesh National Building Code 2020. It reads your open model over the CSI API, computes the BNBC parameters, and writes load patterns, area loads, combinations, stiffness modifiers, mass sources, pier labels and earth pressures back into the model — each with the code clause it comes from shown beside it.

What it does

Computed from your model
  • Computes the equivalent-static seismic base shear and the parameters ETABS needs to reproduce the BNBC spectrum (Sec 2.5).
  • Computes wind pressures and per-storey forces by the analytical procedure (Sec 2.4).
  • Creates load patterns, area loads by storey, load combinations — strength, service, overstrength, serviceability — stiffness modifiers, the mass source, restraints, edge constraints and pier labels.
  • Runs a 10-point pre-analysis audit, then post-analysis irregularity, P-Δ, torsion and spectrum-scaling checks, and reports modal, drift, reaction and wall-share results.
  • Exports foundation reactions, a quantity take-off, a member schedule and a design-basis report, and saves your inputs to a reusable project file.

What it deliberately does not do

Limits, stated up front
  • It never saves your model. Your undo is closing ETABS without saving.
  • It does not run the analysis — you press F5 in ETABS yourself.
  • It does not design anything. ETABS does that.
  • It does not choose R for you — it tells you what the wall share implies.
  • It does not replace your engineering judgement, or your professional seal.

What it checks

Every check names its clause in the log. The third column matters as much as the first two: a compliance tool that does not tell you what it left alone is not much use to the engineer who has to seal the drawing.

Before the analysis

  • Analysis has been run results present
  • Load patterns & self-weight Sec 2.3
  • Lateral loads generated to BNBC Sec 2.5 / 2.4
  • System vs detailing class Table 6.2.19
  • Stiffness modifiers Sec 6.3.10.4.1
  • Mass source Sec 2.5.7.3
  • Diaphragms rigid per storey
  • Base restraints, pier labels supports, wall forces
  • Load combinations Sec 2.7

After the analysis

  • Modal mass participation Sec 2.5.9.2
  • Re-entrant corner (H2) Table 6.1.5
  • Diaphragm forces Fpx Sec 1.7.3.8(a)
  • Storey drift limit Table 6.2.21
  • Torsional irregularity (H1 / Ax) Table 6.1.5
  • Soft storey (V1a) Table 6.1.4
  • P-Δ stability θ Sec 2.5.7.9
  • Response-spectrum 85% scaling Sec 2.5.9.4
  • Member design, columns BNBC Ch 8

Not checked — do by hand

  • Capacity-design shear Ve from Mpr Sec 8.3.8.1/2
  • Shear wall boundary elements on demand Sec 8.3.6.6
  • Computed member deflection Table 6.1.2
  • Deformation compatibility in SDC D Sec 2.5.14.4
  • Site-specific study for site class S1 / S2 Sec 2.5.5.4
  • Sliding on the foundation Sec 1.6.4
  • Time-history analysis Sec 2.5.10 / 2.5.11
  • Wind on components and cladding Sec 2.4.9–2.4.11

A further group — diaphragm discontinuity, out-of-plane offsets, in-plane discontinuity, non-parallel systems, horizontal cantilevers, seismic detailing class — is declared by you in the program, because no model can settle them on its own.

ETABS must run at the same elevation as EtabsX — both as administrator, or neither — or the connection silently attaches to nothing. The Seismic and Guide tabs compute without ETABS; everything else reads from or writes to a live model.
