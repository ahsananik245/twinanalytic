Compliance Check · BNBC 2020 Table 6.1.4

Soft Storey & Stiffness Irregularity Check

Screens for vertical stiffness irregularity types 1a and 1b. A storey is soft when its stiffness drops below 70 % of the storey above or 80 % of the average of the three above; the extreme thresholds are 60 % and 70 %.

Design Parameters

Storey Stiffness (top level first)
Enter the levels in the order ETABS prints them, from the roof down to the ground floor, so that "the storey above" is the row directly above. Stiffness units are arbitrary as long as they are consistent — only ratios are assessed.
