Compliance Check · BNBC 2020 Sec 2.5.9

Overturning Moment Stability Check

Accepts either the storey force profile or the storey shear profile and forms the overturning moment correctly for each. The resisting moment uses 0.9D with the seismic case, in line with the governing load combination.

Design Parameters

Lateral Load Profile
A storey force acts at its own elevation above the base. A storey shear acts over its own storey height. Multiplying a shear profile by the cumulative elevation would count the lower storeys repeatedly.
Resisting System
The lever arm is the horizontal distance from the overturning edge to the centre of mass — half the base width for a symmetric building.
