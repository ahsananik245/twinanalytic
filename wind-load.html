Wind Load Analysis — MWFRS, Any Height

Directional procedure for the main wind force resisting system. Determines whether the structure is rigid or flexible, builds the appropriate gust effect factor, then produces the full windward pressure profile together with leeward and side wall pressures for both signs of internal pressure.
